Information We Collect

User-Provided Information. You provide us information about yourself, such as your name and e-mail address, when you register for a Member account with the Service. When you use the Service to manage your rental properties, you also provide information such as property addresses, rent amounts, tenant contact information (name, e-mail, phone), lease dates, security deposits, and receipt and expense records. Tenant information is entered by the Member who manages that property, not by the tenant directly; if you are a Member, you are responsible for having an appropriate basis to provide your tenants' personal information to the Service. This information is private to your account -- it is not published to the public or to other Members, except that a receipt you generate may be sent by e-mail to the tenant named on it, at your direction.

Sign-In Information. The Service keeps you signed in using a security token stored on your device (in browser local storage on the web, or in secure, encrypted storage on iOS) rather than tracking cookies. We do not use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies for advertising or analytics purposes.

Log File Information. Log file information is automatically reported by your browser each time you access a web page. When you register with or view our site, our servers automatically record certain information that your web browser sends whenever you visit any website. These server logs may include information such as your web request, Internet Protocol ("IP") address, browser type, referring / exit pages and URLs, and other such information.

Third-Party Service Providers. We rely on a small number of service providers to operate the Service, each of which processes some of your information on our behalf: our application servers are hosted on Heroku (a Salesforce company) and our database is hosted on MongoDB Atlas, both on servers located in the United States; e-mails sent by the Service (receipts, password resets, and other service notices) are delivered through Google's Gmail service; and we use Google Maps Platform to display property addresses on a map within the Service, which sends the relevant property address (along with your device's IP address) to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles that information. We do not use third-party advertising networks, ad trackers, or analytics services, and we do not serve advertisements on the Service.

If You Are a Tenant

If your landlord or property manager uses RentChek, they may have entered your contact information and payment history into their account. In that situation they -- not RentChek -- decide what information to record about you, why, and how long to keep it. RentChek stores it on their behalf, and has no direct relationship with you.

That means requests about your information (to see it, correct it, or have it removed) should go to your landlord or property manager first, since they control it and can edit or delete it themselves at any time. You are welcome to contact us at chesley@rentchek.com and we will help where we can, but in most cases we will need to direct you back to them, and we may need to notify them of your request in order to act on it.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep your account information and records for as long as your account is open. If you delete your account, your account record, properties, tenants, receipts, and expenses are deleted from our active database at that time; residual copies may persist for a limited period in routine backups before being overwritten. We may retain a limited amount of information for longer where we are required to by law, or where it is necessary to resolve a dispute or enforce our Terms of Service.

RentChek is a record-keeping convenience, not an archival or backup service, and we do not guarantee that any information will remain available or recoverable. Please keep your own copies of anything you need for tax, legal, accounting, or tenancy-dispute purposes.
